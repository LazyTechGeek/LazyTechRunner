# Phone app guide

The phone app does two jobs. It is where you plan routes and look at your reports, and it can also guide a run by itself if you don't have a watch.

## Home

The home screen has a big **Run** button and four tiles: **Race**, **Routes**, **Heart rate** and **Reports**.
The cog at the top opens **Settings**.

## Starting a run

Tap **Run**, then choose what kind:

| Choice | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Route** | Follow a route you planned, with spoken turn directions |
| **Distance** | Go a set distance, anywhere |
| **Time** | Go for a set time, anywhere |
| **Free run** | Just go. Stop when you like |
| **Treadmill** | Indoors, counted by steps, time or distance |
| **Programmes** | Run/walk interval plans, including a nine-week beginner plan |

You then see a **Ready** screen. It waits for a GPS fix, counts down, and says "Go".

During a run the screen shows a live map with your time, distance and distance to go. Buttons on the map switch between flat and tilted views, and day and night colours. **Pause** and **Stop** are at the bottom.

It's called a run, but walking is fine. Afterwards the app labels each outing as a walk, jog, run or run/walk from your speed.

## Racing

**Race** works like Run, but you pick an opponent:

- **My best**: your own best time on that route, distance or time.
- **A pacer**: a virtual runner at a steady speed.
- **Just run it**: no opponent, to set your first record.

The voice tells you how far ahead or behind you are. Only a jog or a run can set a new best, so a stroll round your race route won't spoil your record.

## Planning a route

Open **Routes** and tap **+**.

1. The map opens where you are. Tap **Set start**, then tap the map.
2. Tap **Set end** and tap the map again. The route is drawn along roads and paths.
3. To steer it down a particular road, tap **Add via** and tap that road. Press and hold any pin to drag it.
4. **Loop back** adds the way home.
5. Switch **Snap to roads** off to draw straight lines, for parks and fields.
6. Tap a turn to check its wording, or to make the voice call it **Earlier** or **Later**.
7. Save it. It is sent to the watch automatically.

Open a saved route for more: **Edit**, **Duplicate**, **Duplicate reversed** (the same route the other way round), **Share** as a file, or **Save map for this route** so the watch has the map without signal.

You can also turn any finished outing into a route: open it in Reports and tap **Save as route**. Routes made this way follow exactly where you went and are never snapped to roads.

## Reports

**Reports** shows your outings by **Week** or **Month**, with totals for time, distance, steps and calories.

Tap an outing to see it on a map, with its pace. From there you can:

- **Correct the distance**, useful after a treadmill session.
- See **Turn timing**: how early or late each spoken turn was.
- **Delete** it. It is removed from the watch too.

## Settings

| Page | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Date of birth and weight (for calories), and the speeds that count as a jog and a run |
| **Your library** | Routes, programmes, records, pacers and heart rate zones |
| **Voice** | Voice on or off, milestones, turn announcements and the turn timing check |
| **Map and display** | Day, night or automatic map colours, miles or kilometres, and what the watch shows during a run |
| **Starting and recording** | Countdown length, GPS detail and step length |
| **Offline maps** | Map areas saved on the watch: add, re-download or remove |
| **Developer** | Simulation, for trying the app from the sofa |
| **Watch and sync** | Whether the watch is connected, and **Sync now** |
| **Help and about** | This guide, downloads and credits |
| **App updates** | Check for a new version, and update the watch too |

### Offline maps

Maps normally load over the internet as you go. An offline area stores the map on the watch, so it still shows when there's no signal.

Open **Offline maps** and add an area around where you are, or open a route and choose **Save map for this route**. Each area shows its size and when it was saved. The watch does the downloading, so do it on Wi-Fi with the watch on charge.

### Turn timing check

If spoken turns come too early or too late on a route, set **Turn timing check** to **Suggest**. After each outing the app measures every turn. Next time you edit the route it offers corrections, which you can apply to the watch, the phone or both. **Auto-fix** applies them for you.
