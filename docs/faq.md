# Questions and fixes

**Is it really free?**
Yes. There are no adverts, no account and no subscription.

**Why isn't it on the Play Store?**
It's a hobby project. Installing from this page avoids store fees and paperwork.

**Does it work on an iPhone or Apple Watch?**
No. Android phones and Wear OS watches only.

**Will it work on my watch?**
It was built and tested on a Samsung Galaxy Watch 6. Any Wear OS 3 or newer watch should run it, but others are untested, so let me know how you get on. A bezel isn't needed.

**Do I need the watch?**
No. The phone app does everything except heart rate.

**Do I need the phone with me?**
No. Once routes and programmes have synced, the watch works by itself.

**The watch says "No map here".**
There's no signal and that area hasn't been saved. Add it under **Offline maps** in the phone app while the watch is on Wi-Fi. Directions still work without the map.

**The spoken turns are a bit late or early.**
Set **Turn timing check** to **Suggest** in Voice settings, do the route once, then edit the route on the phone and apply the suggested corrections.

**The treadmill distance is wrong.**
Treadmill distance is worked out from your steps. Open the outing in Reports and correct the distance. On the phone you can also set your step length in **Starting and recording**.

**The watch and phone show different things.**
Open **Watch and sync** on the phone and tap **Sync now**, or tap **Sync with phone** in the watch's settings.

**The watch battery drains quickly.**
Lower the run screen brightness or switch off **Always on when running**. Check that **Wireless debugging** is off, and that the watch's own Always On Display is off.

**Android warns me the app is unsafe.**
That warning appears for any app from outside the Play Store. Only download it from this page.

**I found a problem.**
Open an issue on the [Issues page](https://github.com/LazyTechGeek/LazyTechRunner/issues), or leave a comment on the [Lazy Tech Geek YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@LazyTechGeek).
