# Voice prompts

Lazy Tech Runner uses recorded clips, not a robot voice. It joins short clips together into sentences, so it can say any distance or time.

## What it says

| When | Example |
|---|---|
| Starting | A silent countdown, then "Go" |
| Before a turn | "In 50 yards, turn left" |
| At a turn | "Turn left now" |
| Distance left, on a route or set distance | "One mile to go" |
| Milestones | "Halfway there" |
| Programmes | "Time to run", "Slow down to a walk" |
| Racing | "Twelve seconds ahead" |
| Treadmill, by steps | "Two thousand steps to go" |
| Finishing | How far you went, and in a race whether you won and by how much |

## Changing what it says

In **Settings → Voice** you can switch the voice off, choose how often milestones are called, and turn off gentle bends and checkpoints if you find it too chatty.

## Music

The voice plays over your music and lowers it briefly while speaking. On the watch, pair your earphones with the watch itself.
